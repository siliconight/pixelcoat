WHOSE VOICE. The walker's font catalog (docs/reference/ in the factory
root), 2026-10-02: "Assign a typeface to an owner." Until this face every
printed surface in the real look was Blue Highway -- a bank's ATM, a
cigarette maker's ad and the law's small print in one voice. Aileron is the
ATM network's and the machine maker's: fascia legends, the topper, the
keypad's CARD.
